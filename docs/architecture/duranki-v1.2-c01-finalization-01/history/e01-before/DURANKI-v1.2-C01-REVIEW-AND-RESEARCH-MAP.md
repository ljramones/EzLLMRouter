## 3. Exact design choices resolving F-01/F-03/C-5

**Common envelope:** the first operation-aware successor includes commission/delegation/activation/absence fields and their standalone ledger representation. This is not Vega instrumentation. A valid empty reason or synthetic value can exercise the contract without an external authority service. Current supported access/handling requirements are never excused by those reasons.

**Binding placement — author resolution amended by Erratum 01 G-01:** caller-asserted commission references, ordered delegation links and caller-declared reasons belong in canonical work. Server-assessed status, verification, coverage and server-assigned reasons belong in an immutable signed admission snapshot bound to the work digest; they cannot change the identity of an identical resubmission. Activation remains in the signed execution context. Lifetime-stable authenticated identity stays mandatory. Later facts append evidence; fresh context never grants replay of a possibly sent attempt.

**Freshness — named mechanism, amended by Erratum 01 N-01:** commit authority records and named revisions durably outside the Choice B first-write fence; publish the coherent, non-regressing sender view under the fence; then acknowledge effectiveness. No database or key-service I/O holds the send lock. Failures close affected release; restart reloads durable authority before sends. The vector is the existing named revisions. This is a successor requirement; it does not rewrite DEV-07’s historical commit-inside-gate description or establish distributed/realtime qualification.

**Financial fixture — author proposal:** Tariff B's rates are invented solely to exercise whole-request tiers and nano rounding while preserving reviewed Tariff A. Their arithmetic is checked as inert data, not a product test result. Full input domain, required usage evidence, independent minima, opposite tie directions and upward reservation are declared in the roadmap.
