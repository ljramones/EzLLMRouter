## 3. What this says about progress

There is documented standalone capability, not merely architecture prose: installed two-language text/tool workflows, live no-store flows, explicit known/unknown outcomes, the separated sender, a local authenticated console, ordinary persistence, model/principal/grant registries and queued revocation cases. Those conclusions are bounded to the dated records linked above.

This is not enough for an honest percentage of v1.1 or standalone completion. Final DEV-08–DEV-10 primary results have not been located in the searched conversation and Library files. Historical executor messages describe later work, but are not silently substituted for receipt bytes. The development-history and retirement files locate those records at a fixed commit; the capture instruction requests them without rerunning the application.

The negative signature/key-version cases and admission pipeline rejection cases need their own proof. Positive frozen builds do not prove refusal of bad artifacts, and valid signed requests do not prove rejection of forged signatures. New E01/successor tests remain unexecuted. None of the mapped evidence establishes real-provider invoices, replicated release fencing, production managed keys or Vega integration.

## 4. Lock-ordering source distinction

Review 04 R04-01 supersedes the blanket ordering proposed in Review 03 N-01. DEV-04/05 supports the fence-protected in-memory revocation/epoch mechanism. The supplied DEV-07 result describes authority data, audit and revision committing inside the first-write gate and the sender view updating before acknowledgement; this is compatible with the standalone single-process SQLite invariant. Preserve that mechanism and its bounded evidence. The outside-lock commit and later fenced publication sequence belongs to PostgreSQL/replicated profiles with network-bound or unbounded persistence and needs separate interval/failure/ordering/restart qualification. No new product run or current-source correspondence is supplied by this document amendment.
