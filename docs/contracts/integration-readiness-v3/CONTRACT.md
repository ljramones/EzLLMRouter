# Duranki external accounting integration contract v3

**Status:** candidate implementation of the owner-adopted VEGA-DURANKI-C01 DEC-01 speech eligibility extension. This document defines only the delta from the accepted [v2 contract](../integration-readiness-v2/CONTRACT.md). It does not qualify or replace the accepted 0.2.2 release. Select `/integration/v3/` with `Duranki-Integration-Version: 3` and `integration_version: "3"` in new events and snapshots. The v1/v2 routes, schemas, records and model-required eligibility semantics remain available.

## Account permission scope

A v3 account has `allowed_models` and `allowed_operations`, each an explicit list. Either or both may be empty; an empty list is never a wildcard, and an account with both empty denies all new selection. The only operation permissions in this version are `speech_input` and `speech_output`. Existing v1/v2 accounts have no speech permission until an owner/admin explicitly updates them. `POST /integration/v3/accounts/{id}/permissions` requires the current revision, replacement `allowed_models` and replacement `allowed_operations`, and increments the immutable account-history revision. The corresponding v3 client methods are `setAccountPermissions` and `set_account_permissions`. A state change retains permissions. Disabling blocks new eligibility but does not reject late observations of earlier work.

## Eligibility selector and binding

`GET /integration/v3/accounts/{id}/eligibility` requires `dataset_id`, `operation_family`, `capability` and `selection_kind`, plus `model_id` exactly when `selection_kind=known_model`. `selection_kind` is `known_model`, `provider_selected` (the request omitted a selector and the provider may choose one), or `not_applicable` (the provider operation genuinely has no applicable model identity). It must be derived from the actual provider request, not inferred solely from an absent field in an upstream wrapper. `capability` is `transcription` for `speech_input`, `synthesis` for `speech_output`, and the operation-family name for other families. Non-speech operations require `known_model` in this version. A known model must be in `allowed_models` even for a speech operation. The speech-operation permission never overrides a denied known model. A model-less speech operation requires the matching `allowed_operations` entry. Account `state=usable` is necessary in both cases. Missing, invalid and mismatched selectors reject; a valid denied answer reports `account_disabled`, `model_not_permitted` or `operation_not_permitted`.

The immutable answer includes dataset, account, account revision, evaluation time, operation, capability, selection kind, any known model, provider ID, credential-manager reference, usable/reason, and an SHA-256 digest over every answer field except that digest. Its ID and exact bound scope appear in a v3 `decision` and captured decision snapshot. The server independently assesses the referenced answer against the retained account revision, provider/credential bindings, operation selector, digest and timing. A decision for another scope conflicts. Caller-reported `verified`, `stale`, `denied`, `bypassed` and `unavailable` remain distinct from this assessment; same-second uncertainty is retained as `indeterminate` under the v2 rule. A v3 attempt start must report `model_identity_state=known` with `model_id` for `known_model`, `unknown` without `model_id` for `provider_selected`, or `not_applicable` without `model_id` for `not_applicable`. Its operation and model scope must agree with the decision in either arrival order.

The decision is observation evidence, not permission for Duranki to execute a provider call. The platform must obtain and enforce a fresh usable answer before its own dispatch. Ingestion and historical reads do not gain send authority. Delivery retries repeat the same observation ID/body and never replay provider work. Result, native usage, monetary cost, corrections, receipt, report and export rules are unchanged from v2. In particular, known native units may accompany unknown monetary cost, and neither a speech handoff nor an account permission adds a charge.

## Boundary

The fixed [examples](examples.json) and [offline checker](check.py) cover the bounded v3 shapes and default-deny representation. Authenticated service and installed-client behavior is exercised by `tests/operator_console/test_integration_accounting.py` and `tests/integration_readiness/speech_v3_installed.py`; the latter runs with a controlled platform speech callback and no real provider request.

This contract stores no audio, transcript, prompt, image or response content. Feature-retention policy remains proposed. The intended real-data activation, operational deployment, Vega instrumentation and Bud actual-binary qualification are separate. Local tests use controlled providers and synthetic identities only.
